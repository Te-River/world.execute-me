A — THE SCREEN IS THE ONLY LIGHT

The room is dark; the page is what lights it. Everything is silhouette and falloff, one cold source, dust in the beam. Canvas is good at light and bad at cartoon, so this stops asking it to draw objects.

B — ONE SHAPE, ONE RED, ALL AIR

Title-sequence grammar (Saul Bass, the same Western tradition the song is written in): a matte field, one precise form, huge negative space, crimson as the only saturation. Directly answers "too many things on screen".

C — DEEP FIELD, BIOLUMINESCENT

An intelligence suspended in fluid: a long tonal ramp, soft bloom, drifting particulate, a figure with no floor under it. The most atmospheric and the least specific to this song.
